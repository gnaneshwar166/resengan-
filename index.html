Rasengan (螺旋丸)

Hold an open palm facing the camera with fingers fully spread. A high-density spiraling chakra vortex will manifest and spin above your palm.

Gesture: Open Palm (Key: 1)

Chidori (千鳥 · One Thousand Birds)

Clench your hand into a tight fist or downward claw grip. Blinding high-voltage blue lightning will crackle, branch, and spark violently from your knuckles.

Gesture: Clenched Fist / Claw (Key: 2)
👥

Shadow Clone Jutsu (影分身の術)

Form the iconic cross hand seal (+) by crossing index and middle fingers, or bring both hands together. Triggers the classic anime smoke explosion (POOF!) and spawns live video clones on your sides.

Gesture: Cross Fingers '+' (Key: 3)
🔥

Fireball Jutsu (火遁・豪火球の術)

Form the Tiger hand seal (index & middle fingers upright) or bring your hand cupped directly in front of your mouth/chin. Unleashes a massive roaring flame tempest and swirling fire embers.

Gesture: Tiger Seal at Mouth (Key: 4)
💀

Reaper Death Seal (封印術・屍鬼封尽)

Clasp both hands together flat in a Prayer seal (Gasshō) at your chest. Summons the spectral Shinigami (Death Spectre) looming behind you with purple ghostly soul flames, cursed kanji runes (封, 死, 鬼), and temple bells.

Gesture: Prayer Hands (Key: 5)
👁️

Sharingan ➔ Kamui (写輪眼 ➔ 神威)

Raise your index finger close to your eye / temple or select from the dock. Your eyes awaken the crimson Sharingan with rotating Tomoes, evolve into Kakashi's Mangekyō, and tear open the Kamui spacetime vortex!

Gesture: Finger near Eye (Key: 6)
🛡️

Sharingan ➔ Susanoo (写輪眼 ➔ 須佐能乎)

Raise both arms high and wide or cross your arms over your chest to unleash full chakra. The colossal ethereal purple chakra ribcage, skeletal armor, flaming horns, and avatar eyes materialize!

Gesture: Arms Raised Wide (Key: 7)
